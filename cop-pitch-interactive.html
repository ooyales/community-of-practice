The Challenge
MicroStrategy is Disinvested. The Question Isn't Whether to Migrate — It's How.
🚫

Option A: Big-Bang Migration

Hire a large team to rewrite every dashboard. Expensive ($$$), slow (12–18 months), high risk of scope creep, and the agency learns nothing — still dependent on contractors the day after cutover.

🤷

Option B: DIY & Hope

Tell program offices to figure it out. Results in inconsistent quality, duplicated effort, tribal knowledge, no governance, and shadow analytics that make the CISO nervous.

✦

Option C: Community of Practice

A structured enablement model that builds organic capability across the agency — supported by professional services that scale up when departments need hands-on execution. The best of both worlds.

The Outcome Contract in Practice
Seven milestones across three phases. Each milestone is inspectable, acceptance-gated, and maps directly to CLINs. The KO authorizes payment upon verified delivery — not upon hours logged. Optional CLINs activate based on demonstrated need surfaced through the CoP, and their milestones provide the same accountability structure. The result: T&M flexibility with FFP discipline.
