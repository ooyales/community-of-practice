The Platform

Why MigrateIQ?

MigrateIQ combines structured assessment, community-driven enablement, and on-demand execution into a single marketplace offering — built for any agency facing legacy BI platform disinvestment.

📋

Structured Assessment

Full portfolio inventory, complexity classification, and prioritized migration roadmap — before a single asset is touched.

👥

Community-Led Enablement

Built-in CoP drives organic adoption. Office hours, showcases, and paired development build capability that outlasts the contract.

🎯

Milestone-Gated Delivery

T&M flexibility with FFP accountability. Hours accrue, but invoicing is gated by inspectable milestone acceptance.

🛡

Zero Lock-In

Every deliverable transfers to the agency. The solution is designed to make itself unnecessary.
